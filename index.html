Fabrica DCM

Fabrica DCM is the internal content tool of De Cero a Motorsport, a Spanish-language education community that helps people start a career in motorsport engineering. It is operated by Matías Écija Fernández (sole trader, United Kingdom).

What it does

  • Publishes and schedules our own videos, reels and carousels on Instagram (@deceroamotorsport) and on our own YouTube channel.
  • Stores and organises our raw footage and finished videos in our own Google Drive.
  • Lets us read and answer the comments on our own posts and videos, and see their statistics.

The tool is private: it is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and only connects to accounts owned by De Cero a Motorsport.
